## Adopt the National Ecosystem Banking Program and Wetland and Coastal Protection and Conservation Act, 2063
### What Ilse Liked:
> Standardized, rule-based mitigation credit framework that prevents commodification loopholes; transaction levy feeding dedicated trust funds; strong in-perpetuity stewardship encumbrances; ordoliberal market design for internalizing environmental externalities.

### What Ilse Disliked:
> Lacks formal institutional mechanisms for cooperative federalism and joint regional co-management; gives unchecked ministerial discretion on baseline credit math without mandatory regional oversight; lacks statutory net-positive ratio requirements (1:1 risk).

## National Ecosystem Banking Program and Wetland and Coastal Protection and Conservation Act, 2063
### What Ilse Liked:
> Establishes a transparent, standardized market unit for ecological restoration; strict equivalence rule preventing fraudulent cross-category offset trading; binding stewardship encumbrances on underlying land even after transfer; direct revenue recycling into the Water Infrastructure and Conservation Trust Fund via a 2% transfer fee.

### What Ilse Disliked:
> Risk of federal overreach without explicit regional consultation in setting credit calculation methodologies; potential speculatory risk from releasing 20% of credits before full ecological milestones are met under Section 3; lack of explicit regional co-management provisions in credit allocation.

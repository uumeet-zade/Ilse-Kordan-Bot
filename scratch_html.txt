</p>

<hr />
<p><b><a href="/wiki/2068_Caprican_presidential_election" title="2068 Caprican presidential election">2068 Caprican presidential election</a></b>
</p><p>The <b>2068 Caprican presidential election</b> was held on 5 December 2068, to elect the <a href="/wiki/President_of_Caprica" title="President of Caprica">President</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Caprica" title="Caprica">Second Republic of Caprica</a>.
</p>
<hr />
<p><b><a href="/wiki/2068_Caprican_House_election" title="2068 Caprican House election">2068 Caprican House election</a></b>
</p><p>The <b>2068 Caprican parliamentary election</b> was held on 5 December 2068, to elect the 20th <a href="/wiki/Parliament_of_Caprica" title="Parliament of Caprica">House</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Caprica" title="Caprica">Second Republic of Caprica</a>. All 33 seats in the House were up for election.
</p>
<hr /><b><a href="/wiki/2054_Caprican_Senate_election" title="2054 Caprican Senate election">2054 Caprican Senate election</a></b>
<p>The <b>2054 Caprican senate election</b> was held on 9 November 2054, to elect the 4th <a href="/wiki/Senate_of_Caprica" title="Senate of Caprica">Senate</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Caprica" title="Caprica">Second Republic of Caprica</a>.
</p>
